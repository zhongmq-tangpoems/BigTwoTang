生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日  |  最后更新：2026 年 11 月 15 日

欢迎使用「唐老二」游戏（以下简称"本游戏"）。我们非常重视您的隐私和数据安全。本隐私政策旨在说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。

一、我们不收集的信息

本游戏是一款完全离线的单人纸牌游戏。我们不会以任何方式收集、传输或存储您的个人信息。具体包括：

  • ❌ 无需注册账号或登录
  • ❌ 不收集姓名、邮箱、电话或其他个人身份标识
  • ❌ 不获取位置信息
  • ❌ 不访问相机或麦克风
  • ❌ 不读取通讯录、短信或通话记录
  • ❌ 不申请外部存储读写权限
  • ❌ 不集成任何分析统计或崩溃上报服务
  • ❌ 不嵌入第三方广告 SDK
  • ❌ AndroidManifest.xml 中未声明任何网络权限

二、本地存储的数据

本游戏处理的唯一数据仅保存在您的设备本地，绝不外传：

  • 游戏进度（牌型排名、成就、游戏内金币）
  • 用户偏好（音效设置、语言选择）
  • 游戏状态（当前手牌、牌堆位置）

这些数据保存在应用的私有目录（/data/data/com.zhong.bigtwowithtangpoetry/），其他应用无法访问。清除应用数据或卸载应用将永久删除这些信息。

三、儿童隐私

本游戏适合所有年龄段用户，包括 13 岁以下（欧盟地区为 16 岁以下）儿童。由于我们不收集任何个人信息，因此根据 COPPA（美国儿童在线隐私保护法）或 GDPR（欧盟通用数据保护条例）等法律，无需特别的家长同意。

四、数据安全

因为没有任何数据离开您的设备，所以不存在通过我们的系统发生信息被截获、数据泄露或未授权访问的风险。

五、您的权利

根据您所在司法管辖区的法律，您可能享有访问权、更正权、删除权、限制处理权、数据可携带权、反对权等权利。由于我们不处理任何个人数据，上述权利不会被触发。但如果您认为我们可能持有您的数据，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

六、政策变更

我们可能会不定期更新本隐私政策。变更将通过更新本文档顶部的"生效日期"和"最后更新"日期来体现。继续使用本游戏即表示接受更新后的政策。

七、适用法律

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 15, 2026

Welcome to Big Two Tang (hereinafter the "Game"). We highly value your privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy explains what information we do and do not collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when using the Game.

I. Information We Do NOT Collect

The Game is a fully offline, single-player card game. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information whatsoever. Specifically:

  • ❌ No account registration or login required
  • ❌ No name, email, phone number, or other personal identifiers
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No camera or microphone access
  • ❌ No contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • ❌ No external storage read/write permissions
  • ❌ No analytics or crash reporting services
  • ❌ No third-party advertising SDKs
  • ❌ No internet permission declared in AndroidManifest.xml

II. Information Stored Locally

The only data the Game handles is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it:

  • Game progress (hand rankings, achievements, in-game coins)
  • User preferences (sound settings, language selection)
  • Game state (current hand, deck position)

This data is kept in the app's private storage directory (/data/data/com.zhong.bigtwowithtangpoetry/) and is not accessible to other apps. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app will permanently remove this information.

III. Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for users of all ages, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Since we do not collect any personal information, no special parental consent is required under laws such as COPPA or GDPR.

IV. Data Security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no risk of interception, data breaches, or unauthorized access to your information through our systems.

V. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing of personal data. Since we do not process any personal data, these rights are not triggered. However, if you believe we may hold your data, please contact us at the email below.

VI. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating both the "Effective Date" and "Last Updated" dates at the top of this document. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

VII. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Game developer resides, without regard to conflict of law principles.
